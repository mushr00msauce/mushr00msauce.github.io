PART 1

Visible

$20 off a month of service with the friend code

STEP 1

Open the referral link

Go to visible.com/get/?63NCPZH. You should see "Join Visible, save $20." If you don't, the code isn't attached — reopen the link.

Tap Choose a plan.

STEP 2

Pick a plan

Leave it on Monthly and scroll to the plans.

Pick the cheapest plan available. Right now that's the base Visible $25/mo plan.

Skip the 5OFF5 banner code; the friend code is the one we're using.

STEP 3

Choose the plan

Tap Choose Visible plan on the cheapest plan.

STEP 4

Tap "Bring my phone"

Don't shop for a new phone. Tap Bring my phone.

STEP 5

Enter the IMEI

In Enter your phone's IMEI number, type 350693901936029 and tap Submit.

STEP 6

Pick a physical SIM, then check out

  • When asked how you want service, choose the physical SIM card (not eSIM). Visible ships it free.
  • In the cart, check the promo box shows 63NCPZH. If it's empty, type it in and apply.
  • Create your Visible account and finish checkout. Choose a new number unless you're moving one over.
When it arrives: send the SIM to me (or bring it with your Mushbox) and I'll set it up in the modem.
PART 2

US Mobile Starter Kit

$6.99 · Verizon + AT&T SIMs

STEP 1

Open the Starter Kit

Go to usmobile.com/shop/product/starter-kit/starter-kit-sim.

The kit is $6.99 and includes a Warp SIM (Verizon) and a Dark Star SIM (AT&T) plus a SIM tool.

STEP 2

Check out

Leave quantity at 1 and tap Checkout. Sign in or create a US Mobile account when prompted, then pay and enter your shipping address.

When the kit arrives: activate it in the US Mobile app and pick the cheapest plan available.
