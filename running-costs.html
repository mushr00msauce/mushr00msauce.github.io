02 · The monthly pieces

What you pay to stay live

The recommended build is simple: two cellular lines through the BELABOX cloud relay — about $60/month for most streamers, on the data plans I help you set up. Everything below the relay and your two lines is optional, added whenever you want it. There's no cap on connections except your budget.

R

Bonding relay

BELABOX cloud SRT + SRTLA relay — the server that merges your bonded links into one clean feed.

~$10PER MONTH
C

Cellular lines 2 recommended

Unlimited data SIMs on different networks for diversity. Two is the sweet spot; add more anytime — the only limit is budget. The plans I recommend and help you set up run about $25/mo each. You're free to bring your own instead, but a line on a major carrier usually costs $50+/mo.

$25+PER LINE / MO
S

Satellite add later

Not needed to start. Add Starlink Roam whenever you want truly remote coverage — from ~$50/mo, or a Mini on an existing account. Pause it in months you're not traveling.

from $50WHEN ACTIVE
$0

OBS hosting — because it runs on your own gear

Managed rigs bill a monthly fee for cloud-hosted OBS. Your scenes run on the streaming PC and home internet you already pay for, so that cost simply isn't on the list. It's compute you already own, put to work — a real slice of the savings, not a rounding error.

No home PC to host on? There's a cloud option.

If you don't have a streaming PC or reliable home internet to run OBS, you can host it in the cloud instead — a monthly service, but it keeps you fully mobile with nothing to leave running at home. I recommend antiscuff.com, which starts at $50/month for the budget package — and you get 10% off with code mushbox
